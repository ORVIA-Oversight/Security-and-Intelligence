## MODULE 04 — Original Evidence, Provenance & Chain of Custody

### Q076 — What is the earliest available original for each material record, and why is it treated as original?

**ORVIA principle:** Observation

**Three-Sides focus:** Evidential Position

**Why this matters:**  
Prevents derivative records masquerading as originals.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Native files; source-system exports; origin records.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a supposedly original item derived from a client-edited copy.

**If unanswered:**  
Log a provenance exception and restrict reliance on the affected item until original-source or custody verification is available. Target the missing support in: Native files; source-system exports; origin records.

**Applicable to:** Universal
