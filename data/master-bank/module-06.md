## MODULE 06 — Their Side

### Q126 — What is the opposing party's complete account of each material event in its own words?

**ORVIA principle:** Reflection

**Three-Sides focus:** Their Side

**Why this matters:**  
Prevents a weakened substitute for their position.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Full responses; statements; interview transcript.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a coherent opposing account omitted from the client's summary.

**If unanswered:**  
Present their strongest available explanation and the outstanding verification separately; missing support does not itself prove the client right. Target the missing support in: Full responses; statements; interview transcript.

**Applicable to:** Universal
