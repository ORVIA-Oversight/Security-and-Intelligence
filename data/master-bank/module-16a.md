## MODULE 16 — Organisational Culture, Governance & Systemic Patterns

### Q376 — What specific recurring practice is alleged, defined independently of the client's individual dispute?

**ORVIA principle:** Insight

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Defines a testable pattern.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Pattern allegation; process definition; event list.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: no common practice beyond superficially similar outcomes.

**If unanswered:**  
Limit the finding to verified individual events; do not generalise a systemic pattern beyond the established sample, denominator and mechanism. Target the missing support in: Pattern allegation; process definition; event list.

**Applicable to:** Universal
