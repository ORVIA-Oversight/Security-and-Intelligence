## MODULE 10 — Missing Evidence, Disclosure & Evidence Recovery

### Q226 — Which primary records should the event ordinarily generate, who controls them, and which are absent?

**ORVIA principle:** Visibility

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Grounds missing-evidence claims.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Record-generation rules; system map; disclosure manifest.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: evidence the expected record was never normally generated.

**If unanswered:**  
Add a targeted recovery or disclosure action with custodian and scope; reflect non-recovery in each dependent finding. Target the missing support in: Record-generation rules; system map; disclosure manifest.

**Applicable to:** Universal
