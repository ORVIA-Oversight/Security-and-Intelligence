## MODULE 12 — Emails, Messages, Meetings & Communications

### Q276 — What is the complete thread surrounding each relied-on email or message?

**ORVIA principle:** Observation

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Restores communication context.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Native threads; mailbox export; conversation IDs.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: earlier or later messages reversing the favourable reading.

**If unanswered:**  
Limit the communication claim to what transmission, context and attribution records establish; recover the missing exchange where authorised. Target the missing support in: Native threads; mailbox export; conversation IDs.

**Applicable to:** Data
