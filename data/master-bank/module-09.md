## MODULE 09 — Contradictions, Inconsistencies & Changing Accounts

### Q201 — Which statements about the same event are mutually incompatible on a material fact?

**ORVIA principle:** Insight

**Three-Sides focus:** Evidential Position

**Why this matters:**  
Identifies contradictions that could change a finding.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Statement versions; event records; contradiction matrix.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a material contradiction within the client's own accounts.

**If unanswered:**  
Keep the discrepancy open with its material consequence stated; do not call it dishonesty without evidence of the relevant mechanism. Target the missing support in: Statement versions; event records; contradiction matrix.

**Applicable to:** Universal
