## MODULE 02 — Parties, Roles, Authority & Relationships

### Q026 — Which legal and natural persons are involved, and what records distinguish similar names or aliases?

**ORVIA principle:** Accountability

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Prevents attributing conduct to the wrong person.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Registry records; identity documents; original correspondence.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a mistaken identity in the client's allegation.

**If unanswered:**  
Keep identity, responsibility or authority attribution provisional; do not attach an adverse finding to an unverified actor. Target the missing support in: Registry records; identity documents; original correspondence.

**Applicable to:** Universal
