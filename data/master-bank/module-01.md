## MODULE 01 — Instruction, Scope & Client Objectives

### Q001 — What decisions must this review inform, and which decisions remain reserved to the client or qualified professional?

**ORVIA principle:** Accountability

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Prevents a review from answering the wrong problem.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Signed instructions; decision brief.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: instructions requiring a narrower or different decision.

**If unanswered:**  
Agree the instruction or scope point with the authorised human owner before relying on it; unresolved scope can invalidate the usefulness of the review. Target the missing support in: Signed instructions; decision brief.

**Applicable to:** Universal
