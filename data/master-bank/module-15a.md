## MODULE 15 — Motive, Timing, Causation & Retaliation

### Q351 — What conduct is established before any motive is attributed to its actor?

**ORVIA principle:** Insight

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Separates occurrence from motive.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Event records; identity evidence; contemporaneous logs.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: conduct not established despite a compelling motive narrative.

**If unanswered:**  
Separate established timing or conduct from unproved motive and causation; remove any unsupported causal leap from the finding. Target the missing support in: Event records; identity evidence; contemporaneous logs.

**Applicable to:** Universal
