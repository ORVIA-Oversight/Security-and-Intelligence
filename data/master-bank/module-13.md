## MODULE 13 — Witnesses, Interviews & Credibility

### Q301 — What did each witness directly see, hear or do, rather than learn later?

**ORVIA principle:** Reflection

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Tests personal knowledge.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Recorded interview; source attribution; event records.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a client-supporting witness relying on hearsay.

**If unanswered:**  
Qualify reliance on the witness or expert on this issue and arrange a targeted, human-controlled follow-up or independent check. Target the missing support in: Recorded interview; source attribution; event records.

**Applicable to:** Universal
