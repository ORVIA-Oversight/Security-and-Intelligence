## MODULE 19 — Public-Source & Independent Investigation

### Q451 — What precise disputed proposition justifies each proposed public-source search?

**ORVIA principle:** Observation

**Three-Sides focus:** Evidential Position

**Why this matters:**  
Keeps independent research purposeful.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Issue map; authorised search plan; source targets.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a search serving curiosity rather than a material evidential question.

**If unanswered:**  
Log public-search limits and unverified identity or relevance; do not treat a failed search, repost or namesake as independent proof. Target the missing support in: Issue map; authorised search plan; source targets.

**Applicable to:** Universal
