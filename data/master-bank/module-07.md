## MODULE 07 — Client Side

### Q151 — What is the client's own account of each disputed event, separating observation from interpretation?

**ORVIA principle:** Observation

**Three-Sides focus:** Client Side

**Why this matters:**  
Captures the client without merging belief and fact.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Recorded interview; client statement; contemporaneous notes.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: original records contradicting an asserted observation.

**If unanswered:**  
Preserve the client account as an attributed assertion on this point; do not promote it to fact without supporting verification. Target the missing support in: Recorded interview; client statement; contemporaneous notes.

**Applicable to:** Universal
