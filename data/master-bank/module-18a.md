## MODULE 18 — Harm, Impact, Loss, Remedy & Future Risk

### Q426 — What specific harm is alleged, by whom and over what period?

**ORVIA principle:** Insight

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Defines impact without presuming causation.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Impact statement; dated records; issue map.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a harm predating the disputed event.

**If unanswered:**  
Keep the impact, attribution, amount or remedy assumption explicit and provisional; avoid diagnosis or unsupported loss arithmetic. Target the missing support in: Impact statement; dated records; issue map.

**Applicable to:** Universal
