## MODULE 14 — Alternative Explanations & Red Team

### Q326 — What strongest plausible explanation supports the opposing account without assuming bad faith?

**ORVIA principle:** Reflection

**Three-Sides focus:** Evidential Position

**Why this matters:**  
Tests the strongest competing interpretation.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Their records; operational context; alternative hypothesis log.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a complete benign explanation matching all key facts.

**If unanswered:**  
Retain the live competing explanation and identify a discriminating evidence request; do not close the hypothesis merely because it is inconvenient. Target the missing support in: Their records; operational context; alternative hypothesis log.

**Applicable to:** Universal
