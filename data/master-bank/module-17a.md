## MODULE 17 — Law, Policy, Standards & External Requirements

### Q401 — Which legal, regulatory, contractual or professional framework governed each issue at the relevant date?

**ORVIA principle:** Accountability

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Establishes applicability before breach analysis.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Official historical sources; contract; dated professional rules.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a different applicable framework than the client assumes.

**If unanswered:**  
Refer applicability or interpretation to a qualified human reviewer; keep any alleged breach provisional until the relevant requirement and facts are verified. Target the missing support in: Official historical sources; contract; dated professional rules.

**Applicable to:** Regulatory / Civil
