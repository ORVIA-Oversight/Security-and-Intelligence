## MODULE 11 — Decisions, Procedures, Rules & Fairness

### Q251 — Which version of the applicable procedure was in force on the relevant date?

**ORVIA principle:** Accountability

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Prevents applying the wrong procedure.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Version-controlled policy; issue dates; adoption records.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a valid version differing from the one the client relies on.

**If unanswered:**  
Mark the procedural step or fairness consequence unverified; separate an absent record from a proved failure to act. Target the missing support in: Version-controlled policy; issue dates; adoption records.

**Applicable to:** Universal
