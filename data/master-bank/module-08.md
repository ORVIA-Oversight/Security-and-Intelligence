## MODULE 08 — Third Side / Evidential Truth

### Q176 — What precise proposition is being evaluated, and what evidence would establish or refute that proposition?

**ORVIA principle:** Insight

**Three-Sides focus:** Evidential Position

**Why this matters:**  
Prevents conclusions exceeding the tested issue.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Issue definition; supporting and contrary sources.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a narrower proposition supported where the client's broader claim is not.

**If unanswered:**  
Leave the affected proposition unresolved or explicitly limited, while preserving independently established components and contrary evidence. Target the missing support in: Issue definition; supporting and contrary sources.

**Applicable to:** Universal
