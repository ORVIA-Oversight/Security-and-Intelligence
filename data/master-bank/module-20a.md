## MODULE 20 — Final Challenge, Human Review & Accountability

### Q476 — Does every material finding contain a precise proposition, classification, cited sources and stated limits?

**ORVIA principle:** Accountability

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Makes conclusions audit-ready.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Final findings register; citation map; report.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: a client-favouring finding without supporting primary evidence.

**If unanswered:**  
Assign the final challenge to an accountable human reviewer; hold or explicitly narrow the affected finding until the material defect is resolved. Target the missing support in: Final findings register; citation map; report.

**Applicable to:** Universal
