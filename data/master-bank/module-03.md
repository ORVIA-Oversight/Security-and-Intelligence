## MODULE 03 — Master Chronology & Sequence of Events

### Q051 — What is the earliest independently dated event relevant to each disputed issue?

**ORVIA principle:** Observation

**Three-Sides focus:** All Three

**Why this matters:**  
Establishes a supported chronology anchor.

**Evidence to seek:**  
Original records; server logs; third-party timestamps.

**Adverse-evidence test:**  
Seek and assess: an earlier event inconsistent with the client's starting point.

**If unanswered:**  
Flag the timeline entry and any dependent sequence or knowledge inference as unresolved; do not supply a precise date by guesswork. Target the missing support in: Original records; server logs; third-party timestamps.

**Applicable to:** Universal
