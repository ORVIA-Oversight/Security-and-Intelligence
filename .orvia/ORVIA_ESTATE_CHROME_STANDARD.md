# ORVIA ESTATE CHROME STANDARD — LOCKED

Canonical visual/reference source: https://brand.orvia.org.uk/#method

This is the estate-wide header, navigation, accreditation and footer standard for public ORVIA sites.

## Header
- Use the Brand website architecture as the master.
- ORVIA master logo must be larger and visually dominant.
- Product/system identity sits beside ORVIA and must be clearly legible, not tiny.
- Main navigation must use the same clean white premium header treatment.
- Dropdown/mega-menu content is site-specific: it must be generated from that site's real page structure and customer journey, not copied blindly from another product.
- Keep a visible Customer Login route where relevant.
- Keep a strong primary CTA.
- Show the ORVIA phone number prominently in the header: 0330 043 3703.
- Phone must remain usable on desktop and accessible on mobile.

## Navigation behaviour
The chrome is consistent; the menu content is contextual.
Each site's dropdown must reflect its own:
- What it does / problem solved
- Who it helps
- Products / services / journeys
- About / trust / boundaries
- Pricing / start / contact routes where relevant

## Accreditation / trust bar
Use the same estate accreditation treatment across every public ORVIA site:
- ORVIA Oversight Ltd — Company 16123685
- Registered office — London EC2A 4NE
- Armed Forces Covenant — Signatory
- ERS Bronze — Employer Recognition Scheme
- Trust A Veteran — current public profile
- Preserve separation between company identity and external recognition.

## Footer
Use the original approved ORVIA footer architecture as the estate master:
- large ORVIA mark
- company/product explanation
- Start Here column
- Method + Products column
- Company column
- Human first. Human last. signature area
- prominent phone: 0330 043 3703
- prominent email/contact route
- company & trust strip
- legal strip with company number, jurisdiction, registered office and policy links
- accreditation bar immediately above the footer

## Visual direction
- Warm, light, premium, corporate, human.
- Larger typography than the current compressed header.
- Clear spacing and hierarchy.
- Do not reduce the ORVIA logo or product name to utility-sized labels.
- Avoid gimmicky AI, sci-fi, neon or robot-led visuals in the chrome.

## Governance
Guardian treats material drift from this chrome standard as WARN or HOLD depending on severity.
No site should invent a new header/footer/accreditation system without an explicit estate-standard change.
